Step 1 of 8

Queue with one signature

Four checks. All on-chain.

The Safe asks Guardian before and after every transaction. No server sits on that path: if every one went down, the protection would hold.

What it says

Risky calls wait longer.

Delegatecalls, owner changes, approvals and upgrades wait up to 48 hours. Batches are read call by call.

How much it moves

A daily budget per asset.

Up to 5% a day is routine, a further 25% waits a day, anything more waits two. Splitting a drain does not help.

What it actually does

Effects are measured.

Balances are checked around execution. A transaction that moves more than it waited for is undone.

What no guard sees

Signatures are firewalled.

Off-chain permits count only once the owners approve them through a delayed transaction.
